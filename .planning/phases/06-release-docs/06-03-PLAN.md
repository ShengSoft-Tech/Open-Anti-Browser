<objective>
Bring the two remaining consumer surfaces into line with the release-notes template: the in-app
first-launch notice Phase 4 shipped, and the dmg drag-install background Phase 5 shipped. Both
currently describe paths that Phase 5's real-hardware capture proved are not what makes the app
usable.

Purpose: D-02 forbids a second version of the 放行 story. The in-app notice sends the user to
System Settings first — a path `05-06-SUMMARY.md` records as never having been exercised. The dmg
background tells the user to right-click and choose Open — a path 05-06 Open Item 2 flags by name
as not even the measured route, and hands to Phase 6. A user who follows either one is being
steered away from the thing that actually works.

Output: rewritten `gatekeeper.step1`–`step4` in both locales, a node:test locking the step ordering
so it cannot silently revert, and two regenerated background PNGs.
</objective>
